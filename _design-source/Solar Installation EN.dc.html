How it works

Six stages, start to sunlight.

Most of the calendar is city and utility review. The work on your property is short.

  1. 01

    Bills and a look at the roof

    We read the usage, walk the attic and the roof where we can, and check the main panel for room before anything gets promised.

  2. 02

    Design and engineering

    Your layout, string-free wiring plan, production estimate and structural detail come back as one package you can actually read.

  3. 03

    Paperwork, in plain words

    Financing available on approved credit. We will help you find a monthly payment that fits, and walk you through your options at your free quote — no pressure.

  4. 04

    Permit and utility filing

    Plans go to the building department and the interconnection application goes to your utility on the same day, so the two clocks run together.

  5. 05

    Install

    Mounts and flashing first, rail and modules next, conduit and microinverters after, then a labelled shutdown at the meter. Most homes are one or two days.

  6. 06

    Inspection and switch-on

    City inspection, then permission to operate from the utility. We turn the system up with you and watch the first production come in.

Money and rules

What is still on the table in 2026.

The federal 30% credit for purchased home solar ended on December 31, 2025. Some third-party-owned arrangements — a lease or a PPA — can still apply a federal credit through 2027. California’s property tax exclusion for active solar energy systems means a new array does not reassess the house. And because SMUD, PG&E and the municipal utilities each value exported power differently, the design decision that matters most is how much of your own production you keep.

Program rules move and each one has its own eligibility test. At your free quote we go through the ones your address actually qualifies for — nothing hypothetical.

Questions

What homeowners ask before they sign.

How long does the whole thing take?+

The array goes up in a day or two. Plan check and utility approval are the long poles, and those clocks belong to the city and the utility — we file early and chase them so they run in parallel.

Will panels fit my roof shape?+

Usually. Hips, dormers and skylights cost usable area, not the project. If the south and west planes are tight we show you what the east plane really produces before you decide.

What happens on a cloudy week?+

Production drops, the house pulls the difference from the grid, and the annual picture is what matters. We size to a year of usage, not to a good week in June.

Do I need to be home during install?+

For the start and the finish. We need access to the main panel and a five-minute conversation at the end so you know what was labelled and why.

Can I add a battery later?+

Yes, and microinverters make that easier than a string system does. If backup is likely within a few years, tell us now and we leave the room for it.
