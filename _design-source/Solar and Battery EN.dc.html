Why together

Panels alone stopped being the whole answer.

What changed is not the hardware. It is what a utility pays for exported power.

Export is worth less than it was

Send a kilowatt out at noon, buy one back at eight, and the trade is no longer even. Storage lets the house use its own production during the expensive hours.

One outage changes the conversation

Solar by itself shuts down when the grid does. With storage in the middle, the house keeps going and you find out during the next shutoff rather than reading about it.

One project beats two

Designed together, the array and the cabinet share conductors, one permit set, one inspection and one crew. Bolted on later, some of that gets paid for twice.

Money and rules

How the programs treat a combined system.

Storage is where most of the remaining money sits. SMUD runs battery rebates of up to $6,000 for qualifying customers, and the Self-Generation Incentive Program continues to fund storage statewide, weighted toward medically vulnerable households and high fire-threat areas. On the solar side, the 30% federal credit for a purchased system ended December 31, 2025, while some lease and PPA arrangements owned by a third party can still apply a federal credit through 2027. California also excludes active solar energy systems from property reassessment.

None of that is automatic. Eligibility depends on your utility, your address and sometimes your household, and we verify all three at the quote rather than after the contract.

Questions

What people ask about doing both.

Is it cheaper to do them at the same time?+

It avoids duplicated work — one permit set, one inspection round, one mobilisation, and conductors sized once for the final system instead of twice.

How much of the house can we back up?+

That is a design choice. Whole-home keeps everything behaving normally; an essential-loads subpanel protects the short list for less hardware. We show both before you pick.

Will the panels charge the battery during an outage?+

Yes, that is the point of the pairing. The array refills storage through the day so the evening starts full again.

Does the battery change how the panels are laid out?+

Sometimes. If most production needs to go into storage rather than out to the grid, the best-performing plane can matter less than the smoothest curve across the day.

Can we start with solar and add storage next year?+

You can, and microinverters make it straightforward. Say so during design and we leave the electrical room for it instead of rebuilding later.
