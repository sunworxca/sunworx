Who we are

One team, from the first call to the last signature.

Sun Worx works out of Sacramento. We are a small, licensed California company, and our team carries the job the whole way: the phone call, the design, the day the crew is on your property, and the walk-through with the inspector at the end.

Questions

Things people ask before they hire us.

How long does the whole project take, start to finish?+

The install itself is short. What takes time is the paperwork around it — the building permit and your utility's approval to switch the system on. Those timelines belong to the city and the utility, not to us, so we do not promise a date we cannot control. What we do promise is that you never have to chase either one; that is our job, and we tell you where it stands.

What happens at the final inspection?+

A city inspector comes out, checks that the work matches the approved plans, and signs it off. We meet them there and answer their questions so you do not have to take the day off. After sign-off, the utility grants permission to operate and the system goes live.

Does my electrical panel need to be upgraded?+

Sometimes. Older 100-amp panels, panels with no room left for breakers, and a few discontinued panel brands all have to be dealt with before a battery or a large array can be connected. We look at yours during the quote and tell you plainly whether it is needed.
